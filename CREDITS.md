# Credits and references

Everything in this repository was written or drawn for it: code, GLSL, motif geometry, sounds and interface. No image, pattern, pin, poster or textile photograph was downloaded, embedded, hotlinked or traced. The references below were looked at as design input only.

## Craft and terminology (facts)
- Wikipedia, "Paithani" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paithani — tapestry technique; kadiyal interlocking; motif names (mor, popat/muniya, kamal, asavali, bangadi mor, narali, chandrakor, rui phool); the traditional colour vocabulary (aboli, firozi, kalichandrakala, morpankhi, motia, neeligunji, pophali, sankirodak, uddani, vangi); history in Paithan and Yeola. The colour *hexes* are this project's interpretation of those descriptions.

## Visual research (direction only)
- Pinterest search, "paithani pallu peacock weave close up" — https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=paithani%20pallu%20peacock%20weave%20close%20up — composition of a pallu: a purple or green body meeting a gold zari field, peacocks in tapestry colours, rani and gold borders with triangular teeth. Taken: the body → stripe band → zari pallu structure and the facing-pair arrangement. All figures here are original geometric constructions.
- Pinterest search, "weaving draft drawdown graphic design poster" — https://www.pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=weaving%20draft%20drawdown%20graphic%20design%20poster — weaving drafts on graph paper, numbered grids and drawdowns as graphic objects. Taken: the loom chart's graph-paper look (fine lines per cell, strong lines every ten) and the idea of putting the draft next to the cloth.
- Shriya's Pinterest home feed — https://www.pinterest.com/ — flat, poster-like colour blocking and small label typography on deep grounds (a vintage print aesthetic). Taken: confidence in a dark ground with a few saturated accents, and small-caps labels.
- Behance search, "textile design archive website" — https://www.behance.net/search/projects/textile%20design%20archive%20website — editorial archive sites where one large floating fabric sits beside big serif type. Taken: the hero composition, with the cloth as the image and a large italic serif masthead.
- Codrops search, "fabric" — https://tympanus.net/codrops/?s=fabric — checked for cloth-rendering demos; nothing relevant was used.
